{props.orgName} ("we") is a private organization run by Airmen in the ranks E-⁠1 to E-⁠4. This policy explains what information we collect through this website, why we collect it, and what you can ask us to do with it.

## What we collect

- **When you give:** your name, email address, the amount, how often you give, the fund you chose, and any tribute details you add. Your card details go straight to our payment processor, Stripe. We never see or store your full card number.
- **When you volunteer:** your name and email address, plus anything else you choose to tell us, such as a phone number, your interests and when you're free.
- **When you contact us:** your name, email address and message.

## How we use it

We use your information to process gifts, send receipts, organize volunteering and reply to messages. We don't use it for advertising, and we never sell it.

## Who else handles it

A few services help us run this site. Each one only gets what it needs to do its job:

- **Stripe** processes payments.
- **Resend** sends our emails, including receipts.
- **Cloudflare Turnstile** checks that forms are filled in by people, not bots.
- Our hosting and database providers store the site and its records.

## Cookies and browser storage

We set one cookie, which remembers your language. While you're on the site, your browser also keeps your donation choices and whether you paused the background video, so they carry over between pages. Those are cleared when you close the tab.

Stripe and Cloudflare may set their own cookies when you pay or fill in a form. We don't use analytics or advertising cookies.

## How long we keep it

We keep gift records for as long as we need them for our accounts. We keep volunteer details and messages until we no longer need them, or until you ask us to delete them.

## Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the information we hold about you. <PageLink href="/contact">Contact us</PageLink> and we'll reply by email. We may need to keep some gift records for our accounts even after you ask.

## Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
