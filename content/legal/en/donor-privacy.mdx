Giving is personal. These are our promises to everyone who gives to {props.orgName}.

## Our promises

- We will never sell, rent or trade your name, email address or any other details.
- We won't share your gift with anyone outside {props.orgName} without your permission, apart from the services that process it. Our <PageLink href="/privacy">privacy policy</PageLink> lists them.
- Giving doesn't put you on a mailing list. You'll get a receipt for each payment, and that's it unless you ask for more.
- If you give in honor or in memory of someone and ask us to let them know, we'll tell them a gift was made. We won't tell them the amount.

## Your gift

- Your gift goes to the fund you chose.
- Stripe handles your card details. We never see your full card number.
- You can change or cancel a monthly gift at any time by <PageLink href="/contact" topic="giving">contacting us</PageLink>.

## Questions

If you have a question about how we handle your gift or your information, <PageLink href="/contact" topic="giving">get in touch</PageLink>.
