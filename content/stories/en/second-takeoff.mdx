Terrence spent twenty years in Air Force logistics, moving people, parts and fuel wherever they were needed. When he retired, he wanted work that kept him on his feet and close to home.

He chose heating and air conditioning. The training went well, but the final course and the licensing exam came due in the same month as a family medical bill.

## The last step

The education and transition fund paid for the final course and the exam fee. A volunteer who'd made a similar switch helped him put together a résumé that translated his military experience for civilian employers.

> Twenty years of logistics didn't come with a license. The grant covered the last course and the exam.

He passed on the first try and now runs jobs for a local contractor.

Gifts to the education and transition fund help veterans train for, and settle into, life after service.
