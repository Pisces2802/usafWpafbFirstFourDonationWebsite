Jaylen arrived at his first base straight out of technical school. He worked long shifts on the flight line, went back to the dorms, and did it again the next day. Three months in, he knew his shop and almost nobody else.

When another Airman suggested a cookout behind the dorms, members voted it through at the next meeting. The morale fund paid for the food, the grills and a few yard games.

## One evening, a lot of new names

Dozens of Airmen came out. Jaylen ended up running a grill next to two Airmen from a squadron he'd never worked with, and they're still the people he calls on a weekend.

> I knew the people I worked with. After that night, I knew my neighbors.

Now he helps plan the next one, and he makes a point of knocking on the door of whoever just moved in.

Gifts to the morale and events fund pay for the cookouts, game nights and trips that bring Airmen together.
