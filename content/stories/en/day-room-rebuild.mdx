The day room on the ground floor of Marcus's dorm had peeling paint, a broken couch and a TV nobody could get working. Most Airmen walked straight past it.

At a meeting, Marcus asked whether the group could fix it up. Members agreed, worked out a budget and got the go-ahead from dorm leadership. The community fund paid for paint, new furniture and a few tools.

## Two weekends of work

About twenty Airmen signed up. They patched walls, painted, put furniture together and hauled the old couch away. Someone brought a speaker, and someone else brought food.

> We walk past that room every day. Now it's ours, because we built it.

These days the room hosts study sessions, game nights and the occasional birthday.

Gifts to the community projects fund buy the supplies Airmen use to improve the base and the local community.
