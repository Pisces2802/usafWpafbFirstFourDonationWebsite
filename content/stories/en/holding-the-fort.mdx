Marisol's husband was four months into a deployment when their youngest was admitted to hospital with pneumonia. Her older son still needed getting to school, fed and put to bed.

Family was two states away. Paying for extra childcare would have meant falling behind on rent.

## Someone who understood

A friend on base sent her our way. The family support fund covered two weeks of after-school care, and a volunteer, herself an Air Force spouse, checked in every few days.

> Someone else who understood military life took one thing off my list. That was everything that week.

Her daughter came home after nine days. Her husband is home now too.

Gifts to the family support fund help spouses and children through deployments, illness and loss.
