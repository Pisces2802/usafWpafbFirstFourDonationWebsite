Dana spent eight years keeping aircraft ready to fly. After she left the Air Force, she found work as a maintenance technician at a regional airport, forty minutes from home.

Three weeks into the new job, her car's transmission failed. The repair quote was more than she had in savings, and there was no bus that got her to the airport before the morning shift.

## One bill between her and the job

Dana reached out through our contact form. A volunteer called her the next day, checked the details, and the emergency fund paid the repair shop directly.

She was back at work that week.

> I'd done everything right and it still came down to one repair bill. That grant meant I could keep showing up.

Gifts to the emergency fund cover the costs that can knock a family off balance: a car repair, an overdue utility bill, a month's rent after a sudden job loss.
