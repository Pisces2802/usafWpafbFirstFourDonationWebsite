Keisha was studying for promotion testing on her own, late at night, from secondhand notes. She guessed she wasn't the only one.

She brought the idea of a weekly study group to a meeting. Members backed it, and the professional growth fund bought a set of study guides the group could share.

## Learning to lead

Running the group meant booking a room, keeping eight to ten Airmen on track, and asking for help when the material got tough. Two senior NCOs from her squadron started dropping by to answer questions and talk about how they'd prepared.

> Leading the group taught me as much as the books did.

The group still meets, and the books get passed on from one testing cycle to the next.

Gifts to the professional growth fund help junior Airmen build leadership skills early in their careers.
